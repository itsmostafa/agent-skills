# Signs of AI Writing

Use this list to review a draft after writing it, not as words to dodge while drafting. Each pattern stands in for a missing fact: rewrite around a specific fact or delete the sentence. One sign is harmless; several in a paragraph make the piece read as filler.

Adapted from [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) (CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Content

| Pattern | Looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Inflated significance | "a pivotal moment", "a game-changer", "part of a broader shift" | Say what changed and for whom. |
| Promotional tone | "seamless", "robust", "blazing fast", "unlock", "supercharge" | Give the number, behavior, or example instead. |
| Trailing "-ing" analysis | "…, making it easier than ever to ship" | Delete the clause or make it a concrete claim. |
| Vague attribution | "experts agree", "studies show" | Name the source, or drop the claim. |
| Scene-setting openers | "In today's fast-paced world…", "Let's dive in" | Open with the point. |
| Formulaic outlook | "the future is bright", "this is just the beginning" | End on the last useful fact or a next step. |
| Summary that restates | "In summary", "Key takeaways" | Cut it unless it adds a decision or next step. |

## Language

- **AI vocabulary**, several in one passage: *additionally, align with, boasts, crucial, delve, elevate, enhance, fostering, garner, intricate, interplay, key (adj.), landscape, leverage, meticulous, navigate (fig.), nuanced, pivotal, realm, robust, seamless, showcasing, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant*.
- **Copula avoidance**: *serves as, stands as, represents, features* in place of *is* and *has*.
- **Negative parallelism**: "It's not just X, it's Y", "no X, no Y, just Z". State the positive claim.
- **Rule of three**: reflexive triplets that make a thin point look thorough. Use the real number of items.
- **False ranges**: "from startups to enterprises" with no common scale.
- **Stiff synonyms**: *utilize, facilitate, commence, endeavor* for *use, help, start, try*.
- **Elegant variation**: "the tool… the platform… the solution" for one thing.

## Social posts

Hook formulas ("Here's the thing:", "Let that sink in.", "🧵👇"), broetry (one sentence per line), engagement bait ("Agree?", "Tag someone who…"), humble-brag lessons, and hashtag or emoji stacks. A good post makes one specific point in the fewest words that carry it.

## Formatting

- Bold on every key term, or bold lead-ins on every bullet of what should be prose.
- Headings on a short post, or in Title Case.
- Tables for two or three facts that fit in a sentence.
- Em dashes where a comma, colon, or parentheses would do.
- Emoji on headings or bullets.
- Markdown where it won't render: plain-text email, most social platforms, commit messages.

## Leaked chatbot text

- "Here's a draft…", "I hope this helps!", "Feel free to adjust…", "It's important to note that…"
- Unfilled placeholders: `[Your Name]`, `(add link)`.
- Invented specifics dressed as fact: a quote, statistic, customer name, or benchmark the source never gave. When you need one, leave a marked placeholder and tell the user.
