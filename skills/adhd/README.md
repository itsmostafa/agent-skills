# adhd

**Replies you can act on without rereading.** The answer comes first, the next step is small and concrete, and the rest is cut or offered on request.

## The problem

Default agent replies open with context, list every option, add caveats, and bury the one thing you needed in paragraph four. If your attention is already stretched, that wall of text is where focus breaks and the task stalls.

## What you get

- **Answer first.** The first line is the answer or result, not a restatement of the question.
- **One small next action.** Something you can start in under two minutes, with real file names and commands.
- **Scannable shape.** Short paragraphs, numbered steps with one action each, and bold used only where a skimmer must not miss it.
- **Fewer decisions.** One recommended option, one question at a time, tangents and caveats cut unless they change what you do.
- **A visible thread.** Progress markers on multi-step work, a one-line recap when you come back, and a nudge back after a side question.

## How to use it

Ask for it once. The style sticks for the rest of the conversation:

```text
Keep your answers ADHD-friendly.
```

```text
That's too much. Just tell me what to do next.
```

## What's inside

`SKILL.md` holds the reply shape, formatting rules, and a pre-send checklist.
